Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to order?

Orders must be placed at least 5 days in advance. This gives us time to bake your cookies fresh with our sourdough fermentation process.

Where is pickup located?

Pickup is located in Del Rey, CA. The exact address will be provided in your order confirmation email after you place your order.

Do you deliver? What is the radius?

Yes! We deliver within a 5-mile radius of Del Rey, CA. Delivery fee is $5. Service areas include Venice, Santa Monica, Culver City, Mar Vista, and Playa del Rey.

How long do cookies stay fresh?

Cookies stay fresh for 5–7 days at room temperature in an airtight container. You can also freeze them for up to 3 months. Just thaw at room temperature before enjoying!

Are your cookies allergen-free?

All of our cookies contain Wheat & Soy. They are made in a home kitchen that may process other allergens. Please contact us if you have specific allergen concerns.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Venmo, CashApp, and Zelle. Payment is due at the time of order confirmation. Payment instructions will be sent via email with your order confirmation.

Can I place a custom or large order?

Yes! For orders over 5 dozen or special requests, please contact us directly at hello@chrisvegankitchen.com. We'd love to work with you.

Feel free to email us or order now and we'll be in touch!
