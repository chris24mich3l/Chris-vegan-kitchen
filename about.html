Our Story

Welcome to Chris' Vegan Kitchen! What started as a holiday baking project in late 2025 has blossomed into something truly special. During that time, I baked and sold over 900 cookies to friends, family, and neighbors around the westside.

Not only did I have a blast baking and packing so many cookies, but seeing my community embrace and support my endeavor filled me with gratitude.

Today, I'm proud to announce I am operating as a certified LA County Cottage Food Operator with a valid Seller's Permit and California Food Handler's Card. Every cookie is made in my licensed home kitchen, strictly following all LA County health and safety guidelines.

Sourdough Fermentation

Every batch is fermented with our sourdough starter. The fermentation process:

  • Breaks down gluten for easier digestion
  • Creates natural prebiotics for gut health
  • Acts as a natural preservative, keeping cookies fresh longer
  • Adds a depth of flavor that balances the sweetness
